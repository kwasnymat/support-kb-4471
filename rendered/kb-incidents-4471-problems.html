parser: day and month swapped

A day-first tenant books 7 April and the invite says 4 July. The shared parser reads every date month first. It hits every day-first tenant, through the app, by email and through booking-v2 forms. This is the bug the queued fix is for.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type month first

Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour run booking-v3. Its date field shows the hint MM/DD/YYYY, so people type month first, and the form then reads the date day first. The symptom looks the same as parser, but the parser fix does not touch it: the hint and the form disagree. Intake tagged these by the reporter's browser, so the hint tag misses some and catches others that are not.

one-day move: the right date, a day early or late

These reports are not swaps. "Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May." "Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday." The desk read them as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) and files them under parser by tenant, so they stay counted there too. A day-month swap cannot move a date by one day, so this is its own bug, most likely in how the invite's time zone is applied.

Why they were merged: decision 0001 merges on subject line alone, and all three problems arrive with the same subjects ("Wrong day on the invite", "Date is wrong"). Subject tells you the symptom. The tenant, its booking form and the body tell you the cause.

How the counts are made

Reports are taken once per id (the export repeats 15 of them), grouped with causeOf in causes.ts by tenant locale, channel and booking form, as the desk's own view counts them. The reports listed in desk-notes.json as one-day moves stay counted where they are filed and get their own row. The table is built from the data at render time.
